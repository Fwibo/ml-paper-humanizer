# Sources of the exemplar passages

The 144 passages in this folder are short excerpts from 30 published papers, kept as style references for writing (pacing, where the claim sits, how pointers fold in). Citations and cross-references are replaced by [cite] and REF, and some macros are expanded. Copyright stays with the authors; each paper's arXiv license is listed below (checked on arXiv on 2026-10-10), and only papers under CC BY 4.0 or CC0 1.0 are included. The skill never reuses their content or any run of six or more words.

License counts: CC BY 4.0 29, CC0 1.0 1.
